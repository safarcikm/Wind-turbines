# Load models

Used for [docs/08-load-verification.md](../../docs/08-load-verification.md).

```
pip install numpy scipy matplotlib
python3 slm.py        # IEC 61400-2 Simplified Load Model (seconds), writes results/slm.json
python3 run_dlcs.py         # doc 08 baseline: 94 design load case simulations (~25 min on 4 cores)
python3 run_dlcs.py final   # doc 11: final controller, 18 m tower, carbon blade, 103 cases -> results/final/
```

- `slm.py`: Simplified Load Model, load cases A (fatigue), B, D, E, F, G, H.
  The equations are reconstructed from NREL reports and must be checked
  against a purchased copy of IEC 61400-2:2013.
- `wind.py`: IEC wind inputs: Kaimal turbulence with IEC coherence on a 9 × 9
  grid (Veers method, longitudinal component only), NTM, ETM, EOG, shear.
- `sim.py`: time-domain aeroelastic simulator. Unsteady BEM with dynamic
  inflow, one flap mode per blade, tower fore-aft mode, rotor speed,
  pitch actuators, torque and pitch controllers, supervisory trips, fault
  injection and the mechanical brake. Also rainflow counting and damage sums.
- `run_dlcs.py`: builds the DLC set, runs it in parallel, writes
  `results/dlc_summary.json` (ultimate envelope, per-DLC maxima, damage
  equivalent loads, power) and `results/dlc_raw.json`, and plots three time
  series.

Time series (`results/ts_*.npz`) are regenerated by `run_dlcs.py` and are not
kept in git.

Limitations (see doc 08, section 3.3): no edgewise or torsional blade
degrees of freedom, no tower side-side mode, no lateral or vertical
turbulence, rigid drivetrain, quasi-steady aerofoil data (no dynamic stall),
no tower drag or tower shadow. The model is not yet validated against
OpenFAST or measurements.
