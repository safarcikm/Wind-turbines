Stall control (doc 31): TSR 5.9 up to 163.6 rpm, then the converter slows the rotor into stall to hold 6.8 kW shaft (6.0 kW electric). 25–30 m/s ramps power down; above 30 m/s it stops on the braking resistor and spring brake and yaws 90° out. Restart below 22 m/s. Speeds 47.5–57.4 rpm are skipped (tower 1P).

Blades are bolted at a fixed −2° pitch; there is no pitch system. Moving rotor speed or yaw by hand switches off "Turbine follows wind". Run switches it back on. Yaw is shown 10× faster than the real 0.5 °/s.
